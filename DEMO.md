# MIRROR — 3-minute demo

## Before the judges arrive

Use a clear, front-facing portrait and keep the app at the landing screen. For a live generation, deploy the `api/` handler and set `GEMINI_API_KEY`. For a network-independent rehearsal, set `VITE_DEMO_MODE=true`; the interface visibly says **DEMO MODE** and does not claim a generated output.

## 0:00–0:25 — The promise

Start on the landing screen: “MIRROR makes the future feel personal. Instead of tracking habits in a spreadsheet, you meet a hypothetical version of yourself and decide which path you want to practise.”

## 0:25–0:55 — Create the reference

Click **Begin your reflection**, enter a name and age, and upload the prepared portrait. Say: “This original image is the identity reference. We never use a generated future as the input for another future.” Continue through the lifestyle prompt with the prepared answer.

## 0:55–1:35 — Reveal a future

Reveal the mirror. Point to the selected horizon and the “hypothetical visual exploration” label. If using the live provider, allow the full-screen persona state to load. Say: “The system creates this as an illustrative simulation, not a health or appearance prediction.”

## 1:35–2:15 — Compare choices

Select **Less active path**, then **Active path**. Call out the deliberate change in story and atmosphere. Say: “Each request returns to the original portrait, while the scenario engine supplies controlled lifestyle assumptions. That protects identity consistency across ages and paths.”

## 2:15–2:45 — Make it conversational

Open the floating companion and ask: “What is one realistic change I can make this week?” Say: “MIRROR turns an emotionally resonant visual into a small, practical next step. It avoids diagnosis and treatment advice.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

Close with: “MIRROR helps you meet the person you could become, then gives you agency to choose the habits that lead there.”

## Judge-ready differentiators

1. **Identity-first generation:** every future independently references the original consented portrait, avoiding the identity drift caused by chaining generated images.
2. **Choice becomes visible:** structured scenarios turn abstract lifestyle patterns into a personal, comparable future-self experience without pretending to predict health.
3. **Trust designed into the flow:** simulation labels, private-by-design framing, server-only keys, validation, loading states and an explicit demo fallback make the product safer and more reliable under hackathon conditions.
