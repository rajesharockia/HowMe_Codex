# HowMe demo visual rule

For the hackathon demonstration, each lifestyle scenario must be visually distinct at a glance while remaining respectful and explicitly hypothetical.

- **Active lifestyle:** show an energetic, fit silhouette, upright posture, fitted athletic or polished clothing, fresh grooming, and brighter light.
- **Current habits:** show a natural continuation of the reference image with neutral styling and posture.
- **Less active lifestyle:** show a softer midsection, more relaxed posture, looser everyday clothing, and quieter lighting.

These details are visual storytelling for a future-self simulator. They are not medical findings, judgments, or predictions that a routine guarantees a particular body shape or health result.
